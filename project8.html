Description

Although im not the biggest fan of typography, I think this project really challenged me to think creatively in different ways than the first idea I settle on. The three prototypes for each sketch might have seemed redundant to me at first but I realized that by forcing me to go through a different perspective I was able to create more interesting works. In my final three iterations I incorporated the camera element with two canvases echoing a previous project, but I was also able to try out the microphone feature in the Weed sketch.
